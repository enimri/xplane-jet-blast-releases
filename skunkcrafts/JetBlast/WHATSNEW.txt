# Shown in X-Plane once after each update (Plugins > Jet Blast > What's new).
# One [version] section per release, newest first, "- " for each point.
# Keep points short: they are word-wrapped in a small window.

[1.9.1]
- Fixed: updating with SkunkCrafts Updater could delete your settings (jetblast.ini) and your own aircraft types (jetblast_types.csv). Updates now leave both files alone. If yours were deleted, Jet Blast runs on default settings and puts back an empty jetblast_types.csv; set your options again in Settings.

[1.9.0]
- Crosswind bends the blast: in a crosswind, blast from far ahead drifts downwind, so a heavy straight ahead may miss you while one off to the side catches you.
- The blast's direction: the debug window shows where it comes from ("18 kt from NW") and the warning names the clock position ("42 kt from 7 o'clock").
- For add-on makers: datarefs jetblast/blast_kt, blast_from_deg, blast_clock, push_g, source_icao, source_callsign, warning and more, for FlyWithLua, sound packs and home cockpits. Listed in the README.
- Send bug report (Plugins > Jet Blast, or the button in Settings): opens the bug form in your browser with the report already filled in. Describe what happened and press Submit. No more copy and paste.
- New aircraft types that download automatically are now announced in this window, once.
- Near-miss log: your strongest blast encounters of the session (peak speed, push in g, source, airport) are listed in the debug window and the bug report, and saved to jetblast_blastlog.csv.

[1.8.0]
- Helicopter downwash: hovering, landing and departing helicopters now blow you around with their rotor downwash, strongest one to two rotor widths away. Marked ROTOR in the debug window; switch it off in Settings.
- 41 helicopter types added, from the R22 to the Mi-26. Helicopters were treated as small airliners before.

[1.7.0]
- Jet blast warning: X-Plane's voice says "Caution, jet blast" and a banner names the aircraft and blast speed when strong blast reaches you. Set the threshold or switch it off under Warnings in Settings.
- VR: the Settings, debug and What's new windows now open inside your headset when you fly in VR.

[1.6.1]
- New look: the Settings window now has a preset bar, ON/OFF switches, - and + steppers (or drag the value left and right), and tips when you hover over a setting.
- The debug window shows the blast at your aircraft at a glance, with colour from green to red.
- Settings now shows a dot in the menu while open; click it again to close.
- Fixed: the debug window couldn't be reopened from the menu after closing it with its close button.

[1.6.0]
- New: this window. It opens once after each update and shows what changed. Reopen it any time from Plugins > Jet Blast > What's new.

[1.5.0]
- Auto Event mode: when 15+ aircraft are within 2 km of you, blast is reduced to Event mode by itself, and returns to normal when the crowd clears.
- It only ever makes blast gentler and never changes your saved settings. Change the crowd size or switch it off in Settings.

[1.4.0]
- LiveTraffic: its real flight phase is used for its aircraft, so take-off and reverse-thrust blast start at the right moment. Marked LT in the debug window.
- Settings per aircraft: in Settings, "Save for this aircraft" keeps settings that load automatically whenever you fly it.

[1.3.2]
- SkunkCrafts Updater support: update Jet Blast from its list. Your settings and aircraft edits are never overwritten.

[1.3.1]
- Smoother at busy airports: ground-height lookups are spread across frames.

[1.3.0]
- Presets in Settings: Realistic, Gentle, Event mode and Off.
- 410 aircraft types, updated automatically from GitHub.
- Blast reach calibrated to ICAO published distances.

[1.2.0]
- Settings window, Copy bug report, and a check for new versions.

[1.1.0]
- macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon) and Linux.

[1.0.0]
- First release: jet blast and prop wash from VATSIM, IVAO and LiveTraffic traffic.
