Jet Blast 1.9.0 beta 4  -  practice mode (beta builds only)
==========================================================

Practice mode lets you try jet blast and helicopter downwash offline, with no
VATSIM, IVAO or LiveTraffic running. It places a pretend aircraft that only
Jet Blast knows about, shown as one of X-Plane's own parked aircraft models.

Menu: Plugins > Jet Blast > Practice (beta)
(every item is also a command under jetblast/practice/ that you can bind
to a key or joystick button in X-Plane's keyboard settings)

  Place A320 ahead of me        its tail 40 m in front of your nose
  Place 777 ahead of me         the same with a heavy
  Place helicopter beside me    a Sikorsky S-76 25 m to your right

  Idle                          (helicopter: sits on the ground, rotor turning)
  Breakaway thrust              (helicopter: hovers 4 m up)
  Take-off power on the brakes  (helicopter: hovers 4 m up)

  Take off and leave            jets roll away and climb; the helicopter
                                climbs and flies off
  Remove practice aircraft

X-Plane's voice confirms each step. The debug window (Plugins > Jet Blast >
Show debug window) marks it PRACTICE and shows the blast at your aircraft.

Things to check in this beta
  1. Breakaway and take-off power behind the A320 / 777: do you get pushed,
     and does the "Caution, jet blast" warning come at the right time?
  2. Helicopter hover beside you: do you feel the downwash (ROTOR in the
     debug window)? Try it with a small aircraft like the C172.
  3. If you have VR: do the Settings, debug and What's new windows open in
     the headset?
  4. Anything odd: Plugins > Jet Blast > Send bug report...

Practice mode is only in beta builds, never in public releases.

Beta channel
  With SkunkCrafts Updater, installing this zip puts you on the Jet Blast
  beta channel: later betas appear in SkunkCrafts automatically. To go back
  to normal releases, install the latest normal zip by hand once.
